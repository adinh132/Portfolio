Hi there! I am an undergraduate student doing a dual major in Crop and Soil Science and Data science. I’m interested in leveraging technologies and our understanding of science and mathematics to better agronomic outlooks and enhance food security. Current technologies offer a multitude of ways that farmers can improve yields, reduce inputs, and improve economic outlooks yet the applications of these technologies have yet to be fully discovered. 

My aim is to provide farmers with the access and know-how to utilize novel data streams, such as LiDar point clouds and field-level spectral data to improve their decision making in a data informed manner. Of particular interest is the identification of novel applications of remote sensing data such as the generation of site-specific predictive models and integrated early warning systems.
